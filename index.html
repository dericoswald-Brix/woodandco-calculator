Interior cost FAQs

Good questions.

How much does home interior design cost in Bangalore?

On this calculator, a 2BHK covering a modular kitchen, living and dining, two bedrooms and bathrooms comes to roughly ₹2.6–3.9 L at Standard finish, ₹4.2–6.5 L at Premium and ₹7.1–12.2 L at Luxury. Adding a third bedroom gives ₹3–4.5 L, ₹4.8–7.5 L and ₹8.1–13.8 L respectively. These are indicative ranges for homes up to 2,000 sq.ft carpet area.

How much does a modular kitchen cost in Bangalore?

A modular kitchen is estimated at ₹0.8–1.2 L for Standard, ₹1.3–2 L for Premium and ₹2.2–3.8 L for Luxury finish, before design changes and site conditions.

What is the interior design cost per square foot?

Indicative finish standards are ₹850 to ₹1,100 per sq.ft for Standard, ₹1,100 to ₹1,600 per sq.ft for Premium and ₹1,600 to ₹2,400 per sq.ft for Luxury interiors.

Do you also work in Mangalore and Udupi?

Yes. Wood & Co. Interiors designs and executes interiors in Bangalore, Mangalore and Udupi, and is an authorised HomeLane Partner.

How accurate is this interior cost estimate?

It is an indicative range based on current market rates. The final quote depends on design complexity, brand specifications and site conditions, and is shared after a free site visit and consultation.
